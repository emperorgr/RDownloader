# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Assume good intent and critique ideas, not people.
- Keep issues focused on the software and avoid posting copyrighted material or links
  to it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained disruption of
discussions are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report abusive behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security/advisory contact. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block
accounts as needed.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
